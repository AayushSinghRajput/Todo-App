About MyTodo

We're on a mission to simplify task management and help people achieve more with less stress. Our intuitive todo app is designed to boost your productivity and organization.

Our Story

MyTodo was founded in 2020 with a simple goal: to create the most intuitive and effective task management tool for individuals and teams. We believe that staying organized shouldn't be complicated.

Our team of productivity experts and designers work tirelessly to refine the app based on user feedback and the latest research in productivity science.

Today, MyTodo helps over 2 million users worldwide stay organized and accomplish their goals every day.
